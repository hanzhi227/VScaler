SeedVR2 throughput on Apple M5 Pro
macOS 26.6.2, 26 GB unified memory, 15 CPUs
measured 2026-09-15 23:11

Method: scripts/seedvr2/bench.py builds the NaDiT block stack at the
dimensions in the shipped Comfy-Org safetensors headers and times real
forward passes through it with random weights (a forward pass does not
depend on weight values). This is a FLOOR on the real thing: the 3D causal
VAE's encode and decode are not included.

MPS allocation is capped at 85% of the GPU working set (macOS reports
~17.7 GB of it on this 26 GB machine) so an oversized case fails in seconds
instead of swapping the whole system.

=== reference: what this GPU does on plain dense matmul ===
  dense matmul 4096^3 on mps float16     25.0 TFLOP/s
  dense matmul 4096^3 on mps float32      5.4 TFLOP/s

############ the other bracket: GLOBAL attention instead of the (4,3,3) window ############
Global attention is quadratic in the token count, which is why upstream
windows every layer. At small token counts it is actually cheaper here --
the window fold has real padding and reshape overhead -- and at the sizes
that matter it does not fit at all. Both are shown.
