External subtitle files — 2026-10-14

UI: Subtitles > Load Subtitle File… (Option-Command-O).
Accepts SRT, VTT, ASS, and SSA. The loaded filename is selectable alongside
embedded subtitle tracks; Off retains it for re-selection. New videos clear it.
ASS/SSA styling is omitted by the existing text renderer.

Loading parses cues off the UI thread, checks that the video still matches,
and rebuilds playback at the current position while retaining pause intent.
Invalid files preserve the current subtitle selection and picture.
