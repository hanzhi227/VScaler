Subtitle discovery and multi-selection — 2026-10-14

Opening a video discovers supported subtitle files beside it and recursively in
subfolders. Discovered tracks appear in Subtitles with relative paths. Discovery
preserves the user's Off setting. The file picker allows multiple files; loading
an .idx/.sub pair together deduplicates its tracks, and selecting multiple subtitle
sets retains all of them. Failed selections preserve the working selection.
