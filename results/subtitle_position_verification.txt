Subtitle positioning — 2026-10-14

Subtitle Appearance now includes horizontal and vertical offsets, a full-frame
preview, and Reset Position. Position changes are applied with Apply and saved
alongside font preferences. Older saved styles decode with zero offsets and keep
their font, size, color and outline.

Text is moved at output resolution and constrained to the video frame. Bitmap
subtitles are moved in the decoder's overlay; display-coordinate offsets are
mapped back into the source canvas before 90/180/270-degree rotation. Original
placement is preserved at zero offsets. Applying position changes preserves pause
and the requested playback time for text and bitmap tracks.
