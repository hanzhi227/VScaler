How to read the budget column: below 1.00x means the model alone would finish a
frame inside a 30 fps frame time; the app also decodes, composites, encodes and
plays audio, so 1.00x is the point at which the model stops being the bottleneck,
not the point at which playback breaks.

Achieved throughput, from GFLOP and ms: 7.10-7.20 TFLOP/s for animevideov3,
6.50-7.35 TFLOP/s for general-x4v3 - i.e. ~7.2 TFLOP/s for both, against the
2.67 TFLOP/s a hand-written single-layer Metal kernel reached in
scripts/bench_realesrgan_conv.swift. That gap is the error corrected in
results/general/realesrgan_feasibility.txt.

Notes:
  * x4 only. There is no x2 checkpoint for either model, so the app's SD cases
    (270p -> 1080p, 540p -> 1080p) fit exactly at 4x, while 540p -> 2160p would
    not, and nothing here fits a 2x request.
  * 640x360 -> 2560x1440 and the rows beyond it are recorded for scaling, not
    because the app would ever ask for a 1440p output; the app clamps 4x to SD
    sources only.
  * CPU rows exist to show that "too slow" is a property of the CPU path, not of
    these models. Any deployment decision here is an MPS/Metal decision.
