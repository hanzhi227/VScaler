VobSub external subtitles — 2026-10-14

Select either .idx or .sub with Subtitles > Load Subtitle File… . The matching
pair must share a basename and folder. The picker starts in the video's subs
folder when present. Languages in the index appear as selectable tracks.

The bitmap decoder uses the existing subtitle overlay before video scaling.
External timestamps are offset to the current video position instead of seeking
past an active subtitle cue. Off, re-selection, and new-video clearing use the
same controls as embedded and external text subtitles.
